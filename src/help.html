Ally Legal Flex

Version 1.0.0 · Maintained by Silvia Shen

Review contracts in Word using your configured chat and embedding models and locally managed policy sets.

Getting started

  1. In Word, open Config → Model Settings to configure and test your models.
  2. Use Config → Company Policies to manage review rules and import or download policies with Excel.
  3. Use Config → Prompt Settings to edit the core prompts for Ask, Summary and Review, or restore their built-in instructions.
  4. Open Ally Legal Flex, choose the answer language, review perspective and policy sets, then use Summary, Ask or Review.

Open Config · Open the task pane

Document features require the add-in to be opened in Word. The 中文使用手册 is also included in the project at USER_GUIDE.zh-CN.md.

Project source

Based on Microsoft's Azure Samples project Ally – AI Contract Assistant, by Esther Dediashvili, Lior Armiev and Andrew Mackay. Ally Legal Flex adds configurable models, local policy sets, review perspectives and revised policy citations.
